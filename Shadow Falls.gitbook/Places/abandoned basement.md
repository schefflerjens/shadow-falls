# Location Profile: abandoned basement

## Narrative Purpose
- **Significance**: Major setting in the Shadow Falls series.
- **Atmosphere**: Sleek film noir detective novel tone, cynical, wisecracking, tough.

## Details
The basement is located on Maple Lane, beneath a hardware shop that went out of business years ago. The place is dark and abandoned, an unpleasant and gloomy place. Stairs lead down to the basement from an alley. There is no other way out, because the door to the abandoned store is shut and blocked by clutter and rubble. The entire basement is filled with clutter, rubble, and various rusty tools that the old business must have failed to smell. Ventilation is poor, the air is stagnant and smells musty. One can spot the occasional rat droppings, though there are no rodents to be seen.
