# Location Profile: grandma's hut

## Narrative Purpose
- **Significance**: Major setting in the Shadow Falls series.
- **Atmosphere**: Sleek film noir detective novel tone, cynical, wisecracking, tough.

## Details
The ramshackle hut occupied by Mama Marie, Charlaine's grandmother. Mama Marie is a voodoo priestess, so the inside is filled with a variety of objects that would fit that pattern. The house is illuminated by candlelight. Under the front porch, Mama Marie's hound Cerberus is waiting to protect his mistress from any physical harm.
