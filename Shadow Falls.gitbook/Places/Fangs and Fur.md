# Location Profile: Fangs and Fur

## Narrative Purpose
- **Significance**: Major setting in the Shadow Falls series.
- **Atmosphere**: Sleek film noir detective novel tone, cynical, wisecracking, tough.

## Details
Fangs and Fur is a country and western bar, one of the busiest places after dusk. Behind the swinging double doors, there's a warm wave of laughter and the unmistakable twang of a steel guitar. The rustic barroom is bathed in the honeyed glow of oil lamps. Worn wooden booths, some with saddles instead of chairs, lined the walls adorned with dusty animal trophies. Millie, the bartender, a woman whose weathered face held the warmth of a thousand sunsets, stood behind the counter, wipes a glass with a practiced hand. Her smile, etched with the lines of a life well-lived, crinkles at the corners as she greets any newcomers. "Welcome to Fangs and Fur, honey. What'll it be?" Fangs and Fur wasn't just a bar; it was a haven, a neutral ground where fangs were bared only in friendly grins and fur ruffled only in playful pats. Here, differences were not a threat but a source of fascinating stories swapped over steaming mugs and plates of Millie's legendary chili. Predators and prey shared barstools, werewolves nursed beers beside hunters, and a vampire was having an animated religious discussion with a catholic nun.
