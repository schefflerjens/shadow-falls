# Location Profile: Shadow Falls

## Narrative Purpose
- **Significance**: The setting — a small town with a supernatural population living alongside humans. Jam's home, his beat, and his responsibility.
- **Atmosphere**: Scorching summer heat. Humidity. Small-town charm with a dark underbelly. Maple Lane is the main drag.

## Key Locations

### Maple Lane
- The main street. Bakes in the afternoon sun. Asphalt goes soft in the heat.
- **Mrs. Peabody's General Store**: Corner store on Maple Lane. Stocks more than lottery tickets. Has a roadside area out front where kids set up lemonade stands.
- **The Bean There**: Coffee shop on Maple Lane. The scent of freshly brewed coffee carries down the street.
- **Sheriff's Station**: Inside City Hall. Smells like burnt coffee and old paper. Has a small mirror on the wall beside the coat rack. Charlaine keeps her voodoo kit on a shelf there.
- **Ol' Macdonald's Farm**: Generational farm fresh produce stand.
- **The Bandstand**: Town square bandstand, shade and quiet.
- **Old Train Station**: Creaky, ghostly echoes of late-night whistle blows.

### City Hall
- Two floors. Staircase creaks on the 4th and 7th step.
- **Anna's Office**: Frosted glass door with name stenciled in black inside. The office is a disaster — medical journals stacked on the windowsill, case files on the credenza, city budget printout on the floor with coffee rings. Framed city charter hangs crooked on the wall. Brass desk lamp throws a tight circle of light. Good leather chair, worn soft.

### Abandoned Hardware Store
- Shuttered for years. One way in through a basement stairwell. Interior door blocked by collapsed shelving and junk. The basement is full of rusted pipe fittings, collapsed shelving units, old paint cans furred with dust. Dark, stagnant air, smells of old dust and mildew.

### Main Street Mini Storage
- Squat concrete building on the edge of town. Where the latest victim was found.

### Dunmore Street (West End)
- Krabat's shop is here, wedged between a watch repair and a closed-down pharmacy. Sign reads OPEN in faded red letters — it's always open.
