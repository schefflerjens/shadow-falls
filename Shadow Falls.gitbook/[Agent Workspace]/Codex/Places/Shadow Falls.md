# Location Profile: Shadow Falls

## Narrative Purpose
- **Significance**: The setting — a small town with a supernatural population living alongside humans. Jam's home, his beat, and his responsibility.
- **Atmosphere**: Scorching summer heat. Humidity. Small-town charm with a dark underbelly. Maple Lane is the main drag — Mrs. Peabody's General Store, The Bean There cafe, a bandstand in the square.

## Sensory Details
- **Sight**: Main Street with its familiar storefronts. The train station, Ol' Macdonald's farm stand. The Sheriff's station. City Hall. Rooftops for chasing perps. A community with visible supernatural residents.
- **Sound**: Crickets, rustling leaves, coffee grinding at The Bean There, Mrs. Peabody calling out daily specials, the train whistle.
- **Smell & Taste**: Freshly brewed coffee, baked goods, hot pavement, lemonade from roadside stands, the metallic tang of blood and ozone after magical events.
- **Touch**: Oppressive heat, dry air, the rough texture of clay skin cracking in the sun.
