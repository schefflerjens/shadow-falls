# Location Profile: Fangs and Fur

## Narrative Purpose
- **Significance**: The local bar where Charlaine and Krabat vanished. A neutral ground where humans and supernaturals mix. Key clue location (the 50th-anniversary rosettes).
- **Atmosphere**: Warm lantern light, twangy country music, worn wooden tables. A rough-and-tumble saloon. Patrons include humans, shifters, trolls, and other supernaturals. Millie the bartender runs the place.

## Sensory Details
- **Sight**: Flickering lanterns, wooden floorboards, a stage for music, crowded tables. A mix of patrons — shifters with subtle changes, a hulking troll nursing ale.
- **Sound**: Country music, chatter, clinking glasses, fights brewing.
- **Smell & Taste**: Alcohol (Tequila, whiskey), Millie's espresso machine (surprisingly), sweat, the metallic tang of ozone and clove after the disappearance.
- **Touch**: Sticky bar tops, worn wood, the cold glass of a shot.
