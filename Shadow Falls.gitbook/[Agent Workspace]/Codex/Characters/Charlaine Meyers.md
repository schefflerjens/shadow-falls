# Character Profile: Charlaine Meyers

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Sidekick, deputy, exposition bridge to voodoo/magic
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: Torn between her mundane law enforcement job and her family's voodoo heritage. Her grand-mère was a mambo (voodoo priestess), and she carries the tools even if she's not fully practicing.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Dark hair, sharp eyes. Tends toward practical police attire. Carries a worn pouch with voodoo trinkets and herbs from her grandmother.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Speaks with a Creole/French cadence ("mon cher...iff", "la douleur exquise"). More careful and methodical than Jam. Gum-chewer.
- **Internal Vibe**: Competent but nervous. Knows more about the supernatural than she lets on. Respects Jam deeply but isn't afraid to tease him.

## Background & Motivation
- **History**: Raised in Shadow Falls by her grand-mère, a voodoo mambo. Joined the Sheriff's department. Has a tracking kit with lavender dust, herbs, and charms.
- **Goal**: Solve the case, protect the town, survive the magical mess they've stumbled into
- **Fatal Flaw**: Underestimates her own magical knowledge and instincts; defers to Jam when she should speak up
