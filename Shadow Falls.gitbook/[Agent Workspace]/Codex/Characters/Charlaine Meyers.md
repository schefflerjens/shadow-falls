# Character Profile: Charlaine Meyers

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Sidekick, deputy, exposition bridge to voodoo/magic
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: Torn between her mundane law enforcement job and her family's voodoo heritage. Her grand-mère (Grandmother) was a mambo (voodoo priestess), and she carries the tools even if she's not fully practicing.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Dark hair, sharp eyes. Tends toward practical police attire. Often has a pencil tucked behind her ear and another in her hand. Carries a worn canvas bag on her shoulder — her grandmother's voodoo kit — that clinks when she shifts it.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Speaks with a Creole cadence ("Grand-Mère," "amour non partagé," "messieurs"). More careful and methodical than Jam. Chews gum when thinking. Quietly observant. Uses humor to needle Jam — especially about his obvious feelings for Anna.
- **Internal Vibe**: Competent but nervous. Knows more about the supernatural than she lets on. Has already decided to be worried and is just waiting for Jam to give her a reason. Deeply loyal. Respects Jam deeply but isn't afraid to mock him.

## Key Relationships
- **Jam Slate**: Playful teasing (calls him out on his bullshit), deep loyalty. She's the only one who can get away with mocking him. Their banter is a sign of trust. She says "Love you, too" when he grumbles about insubordination.
- **Grand-Mère** (Mama Marie): Her grandmother, a voodoo mambo. Papa Legba is "Grand-Mère's domain" — not something Charlaine messes with.
- **Anna Arborio**: Respectful professional relationship.
- **Krabat**: Target of suspicion. Dislikes and distrusts him.
