# Character Profile: Papa Legba / Eshu

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Trickster god, antagonist/ally, patron of crossroads
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: A voodoo Loa (god) of crossroads, gateways, and trickery. His motives are opaque. He may be behind the crisis, or he may be opposing whoever is. He operates from the spirit world and cannot act directly in the physical realm without intermediaries.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Appears as "Eshu" — tall, lean. Wears a long black tattered coat that moves in a wind that isn't blowing. A wide-brimmed straw hat casting an absolute shadow where a face should be. Carries a gnarled walking stick. His voice is raspy, old, patient in a way that feels geological rather than human.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Cryptic, amused, patient. "Names have power for me and my kin." Calls himself Eshu. Objects to being called "Daddy Longlegs." Speaks in precise, deliberate sentences. Does not lie but does not volunteer. His silence is as calculated as his speech.
- **Internal Vibe**: Ancient, patient, dangerous — not malevolent, but not benevolent either. He tests people. He deals in passage and crossing, not in murder. Refers to Jam as being made of "old earth" — the oldest kind, shaped with intention and given a name and purpose.

## Domain
- Guardian of crossroads, gateways, the spaces between. Charlaine describes him as "Grand-Mère's domain" — not to be joked about.
- Cannot act directly in the physical world. Can only work through intermediaries.
- Can manifest at intersections of life and death (a murder scene, a vampire).
- The spirit world is his domain — a void with pewter-colored light, a shimmering membrane at its edges. Guarded by a giant red-eyed hound that has a name Legba will not give.

## The Deal (Chapter 4)
- Someone has been using Legba's old architecture/workings to tear the membrane between the spirit world and the physical world. Every working leaves a scar. Enough scars and the membrane fails.
- Legba is invested in stopping this.
- He offered Jam a deal: find who is using the workings, stop them, and bring Legba the anchor/instrument they've been using to tear the membrane.
- In return, Legba will reveal the location of the woman in a sepia photograph he produced — a woman Jam has been searching for for 40 years.
- Legba gave Jam a "Jack of Crossroads" playing card to allow crossing between worlds.

## Key Items
- **Jack of Crossroads**: A playing card Legba gave Jam. Not standard — shows a man at a fork in a road looking both ways. Warm. Allows crossing.
- **Sepia Photograph**: Shows a woman Jam has been looking for for 40 years. Legba used it as leverage.

## The Hound
- A giant red-eyed hound that guards the spirit world. The size of a draft horse. Coat like midnight with a shine like wet ink. Silent movement. Red eyes lit from behind the iris, like coals in the dark. Has a name Legba refuses to give.

## Key Relationships
- **Jam Slate**: Made a deal. Jam is the right tool for this job — made of old earth, can trace workings in ways living people cannot.
- **Mama Marie (Grand-Mère)**: Follower. Her family line serves him.
- **Charlaine Meyers**: Protected indirectly through Grand-Mère's connection. Currently in Krabat's possession (revealed at the end of Ch4).
