# Character Profile: Krabat (Šajatović)

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Reluctant ally, dark mage, red herring
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: A powerful European mage whose magic is rooted in different traditions than voodoo. Knows more than he says. Hated by Jam, tolerated by Anna.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: White hair, black eyes. Tall, graceful, unsettling movements. Speaks with a silky, predatory cadence. Boots click on wooden floors.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Sarcastic, evasive, condescending. Calls Charlaine "Charly." Enjoys getting under Jam's skin. Dislikes being called "Šajatović" (his birth name, which historians uncovered).
- **Internal Vibe**: Dangerous, manipulative, but ultimately not the villain. A collector of relics and secrets.

## Background & Motivation
- **History**: European mage, centuries old (implied). Anna hired him for research. His magic is continental European tradition — different from voodoo entirely. Collects magical relics.
- **Goal**: Unknown — self-preservation, possibly protecting his own interests
- **Fatal Flaw**: Arrogance, evasiveness. Nobody fully trusts him, which makes him an unreliable ally.
