# Character Profile: Krabat (Šajatović)

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Reluctant ally, dark mage, red herring
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: A powerful European mage whose magic is rooted in different traditions than voodoo. Knows more than he says. Hated by Jam, tolerated by Anna. **Appears to be the mastermind but is actually being framed or manipulated by the real antagonist.**

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: White hair, black eyes. Tall, graceful, unsettling movements. Speaks with a silky, predatory cadence. Boots click on wooden floors.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Sarcastic, evasive, condescending. Calls Charlaine "Charly." Enjoys getting under Jam's skin. Dislikes being called "Šajatović" (his birth name). Chooses words carefully — always sounds like he knows more than he's saying.
- **Internal Vibe**: Dangerous, manipulative, but ultimately not the villain. A collector of relics and secrets. Has been three steps ahead of Jam the whole time — not because he's running the operation, but because he's being used as a pawn and knows it.

## Location
- **Shop**: Dunmore Street (west end of town), wedged between a watch repair and a closed-down pharmacy. The sign in the window says OPEN in faded red letters. It always says open — no one has ever known him to close.

## The Cabin Setup
- Krabat set up the cabin in the woods as a holding location for Charlaine, but he wasn't there when Jam arrived. The constructs, the underground chamber, the bone-disk anchor were all there, but he'd left. Jam found the anchor and took it — which may have been exactly what Krabat (or whoever is pulling his strings) wanted.

## Key Relationships
- **Jam Slate**: Mutual disdain. Hostile, distrustful. Jam does not trust Krabat but needs his knowledge.
- **Anna Arborio**: Employer / Consultant. Does research for her. She insists he's a resource, not her "Igor."
- **Charlaine Meyers**: Uneasy respect — she gets a nod from him when others don't.
