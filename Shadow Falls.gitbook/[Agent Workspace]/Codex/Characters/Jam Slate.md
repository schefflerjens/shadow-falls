# Character Profile: Jam Slate

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Protagonist, first-person narrator, town Sheriff
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: External — tracking a serial killer with magical backing. Internal — a golem made of clay who's literally drying out in the heat, grappling with mortality and the limits of his own nature.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Clay-skinned golem Sheriff. Wears a hat, badge, and standard police uniform. His clay skin crackles in heat, dries out, and can break. Missing fingers regenerate when rehydrated. Beard is sculpted, not grown. The clay has a dull resistance when stiffening. When bullets hit him, they sink into the clay and stop.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Cynical, wisecracking, tough. Film noir detective cadence. Short sentences, punchy dialogue. Tends toward sarcasm. Calls everyone by name. Uses "ain't" and contractions freely. Moves carefully when joints are stiff — acutely aware of every seam and crack.
- **Internal Vibe**: World-weary but stubborn. Been Sheriff for 60+ years. Has buried too many people he cared about. Won't let Charlaine ride backup on dangerous scenes because he doesn't want her name on that list. Doesn't swallow when drinking — moisture absorbs through palms, lips, face. Caffeine works faster in clay than blood.

## Background & Motivation
- **History**: A golem created over a century ago to protect Shadow Falls. Has served as Sheriff for at least 60 years. Carries a garlic clove in a ziploc bag for breaking up bar fights at Fangs and Fur (has done so since a fight on Clement Street in 1987). Has known Anna Arborio for 30 years.
- **Goal**: Catch the serial killer, rescue Charlaine and Krabat, keep Shadow Falls safe
- **Fatal Flaw**: Pride — insists on doing things himself ("It's gotta be me"). Reluctant to ask for help or call in outside agencies. Has a history with the Bureau trying to confiscate him. Physically vulnerable to heat/dehydration.
