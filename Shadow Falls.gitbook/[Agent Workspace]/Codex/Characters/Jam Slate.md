# Character Profile: Jam Slate

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Protagonist, first-person narrator, town Sheriff
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: External — tracking a serial killer with magical backing. Internal — a golem made of clay who's literally drying out in the heat, grappling with mortality and the limits of his own nature.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Clay-skinned golem Sheriff. Wears a hat, badge, and standard police uniform. His clay skin crackles in heat, dries out, and can break. Missing fingers regenerate when rehydrated. Beard is sculpted, not grown.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Cynical, wisecracking, tough. Film noir detective cadence. Short sentences, punchy dialogue. Tends toward sarcasm. Calls everyone by name. Uses "ain't" and contractions freely.
- **Internal Vibe**: World-weary but stubborn. Been keeping Shadow Falls safe for over a century. Genuinely cares about the town's people but won't admit it.

## Background & Motivation
- **History**: A golem created over a century ago to protect Shadow Falls. Has served as Sheriff for decades. Survives on moisture — coffee is his preferred hydration. Vulnerable to heat that dries his clay. Religion and voodoo give him "the heebie jeebies."
- **Goal**: Catch the serial killer, rescue Charlaine and Krabat, keep Shadow Falls safe
- **Fatal Flaw**: Pride — insists on doing things himself ("It's gotta be me"). Reluctant to ask for help or call in outside agencies. Physically vulnerable to heat/dehydration.
