# Character Profile: Anna Arborio

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Mayor, ally, voice of reason, vampire
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: A vampire who's been sober for 50 years (AA chip). Balances her supernatural nature with her public role as mayor. The blood-drinking urge is a constant battle.

## Background
- Anna came across the ocean from the Old World before Shadow Falls was founded. She has been a vampire longer than most towns in this country have existed.
- Her age is unknown and deliberately obscured. She appears as a tired, pale woman in formal suits, but her power comes not just from wealth — it comes from age. Vampires grow stronger as they age, and Anna is very, very old.
- She has known Jam for 30 years (a brief friendship by her measure). She has been mayor for longer than anyone remembers. The city coffers are notoriously empty — her personal wealth keeps Shadow Falls afloat. Without her, the town would be dirt.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Pale, tired-looking. Wears reading glasses. Dark hair pinned up with a letter opener. Dressed in formal suits. Her office is a disaster — medical journals stacked on the windowsill, case files on the credenza, city budget printouts on the floor with coffee rings. A framed city charter hangs crooked on the wall behind her desk. A brass desk lamp throws a tight circle of yellow light over the clutter.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Clipped, even authority. Lets people finish speaking before responding. Knows when she's won an argument and moves on. Practical to a fault. Carries a flask of pig's blood for vampiric cravings. Says "one day at a time."
- **Internal Vibe**: Exhausted, hiding the strain beneath an ancient patience. Genuinely cares about Jam and the town.

## Key Relationships
- **Jam Slate**: Sponsor/sponsee (AA), mayor/sheriff, old friends. She's the closest thing to family Jam has. She personally funded the legal fees that kept the federal government from confiscating him.
- **The Photograph (The Woman)**: Anna knows who the woman in the sepia photograph is. She didn't ask "who" — she asked "forty years?" as a statement of recognition. She has been carrying this knowledge alongside Jam for decades.
- **Mama Marie**: Fellow old-world expatriate. They share an understanding that predates Shadow Falls.
- **Krabat**: Hired him for research. Insists he's not her "Igor."
