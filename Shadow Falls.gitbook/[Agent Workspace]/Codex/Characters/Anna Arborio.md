# Character Profile: Anna Arborio

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Mayor, ally, voice of reason, vampire
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: A vampire who's been sober for 50 years (AA chip). Balances her supernatural nature with her public role as mayor. The blood-drinking urge is a constant battle.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Pale, tired-looking. Wears sunglasses to hide bloodshot eyes. Messy office piled with medical journals and pill bottles mixed with municipal paperwork.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Motherly but firm. Pragmatic. Carries a flask of pig's blood for vampiric cravings. Dark circles under eyes — hasn't slept since the killings started. Says "one day at a time."
- **Internal Vibe**: Exhausted, hiding the strain. Genuinely cares about Jam and the town.

## Background & Motivation
- **History**: Vampire, 50 years sober. Mayor of Shadow Falls. Also acts as town physician when needed. Jam's AA sponsor — they have a "one day at a time" pact.
- **Goal**: Keep Shadow Falls safe, manage the crisis without the supernatural world exposing itself, stay sober
- **Fatal Flaw**: Her vampire nature makes her vulnerable in daylight. She's spread thin between governing and the crisis.
