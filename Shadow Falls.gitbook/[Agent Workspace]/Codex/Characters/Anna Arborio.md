# Character Profile: Anna Arborio

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Mayor, ally, voice of reason, vampire
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: A vampire who's been sober for 50 years (AA chip). Balances her supernatural nature with her public role as mayor. The blood-drinking urge is a constant battle.

## Appearance & Mannerisms
- **Physical Description**: Pale, tired-looking. Wears reading glasses. Dark hair pinned up with a letter opener. Dressed in formal suits. Her office is a disaster — medical journals stacked on the windowsill, case files on the credenza, city budget printouts on the floor with coffee rings. A framed city charter hangs crooked on the wall behind her desk. A brass desk lamp throws a tight circle of yellow light over the clutter.
- **Mannerisms & Speech**: Clipped, even authority. Lets people finish speaking before responding. Knows when she's won an argument and moves on. Practical to a fault. Carries a flask of pig's blood for vampiric cravings. Says "one day at a time." Has known Jam for 30 years — she assesses him with practiced clinical distance.
- **Internal Vibe**: Exhausted, hiding the strain. Genuinely cares about Jam and the town. The city coffers are notoriously empty — her personal wealth keeps Shadow Falls afloat. Without her, the town would be dirt.

## Key Relationships
- **Jam Slate**: Sponsor/sponsee (AA), mayor/sheriff, old friends. She's the closest thing to family Jam has. She worries. He dismisses her worry. She personally funded the legal fees that kept the federal government from confiscating him. "This town has spent a lot to keep you here."
- **The Photograph (The Woman)**: Anna knows who the woman in the sepia photograph is — the woman Jam has been searching for for 40 years. When Jam told her about Legba's deal, she didn't ask "who" — she asked "forty years?" as a statement of recognition. The identity of the woman is not revealed in the current story, but Anna has been carrying this knowledge alongside Jam for decades.
- **Charlaine Meyers**: Respectful professional relationship.
- **Krabat**: Hired him for research. Insists he's not her "Igor."
