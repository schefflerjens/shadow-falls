# Lore: Voodoo & the Spirit World

## Description & History
- **Summary**: Voodoo is an active magical tradition in Shadow Falls, rooted in the practices of the Haitian/Creole diaspora. It involves Loa (spirits/gods), rituals, talismans, and communication with the spirit world. Charlaine's grand-mère was a mambo (priestess).
- **History & Origin**: Brought to Shadow Falls by Creole families. The Meyers family has maintained the tradition across generations. Papa Legba / Eshu is a central Loa — the gatekeeper of crossroads and the spirit world.

## Rules & Mechanics
- **Loa**: Gods/spirits that can be called upon, bargained with, or angered. Papa Legba operates at the crossroads between worlds.
- **The Spirit World**: A parallel dimension accessible via summoning circles. Appears as a vast, empty void with shimmering walls. Guarded by giant red-eyed hounds. Time and space work differently there.
- **Tracking Dust**: Lavender-colored shimmering dust that follows magical traces. Used by Charlaine.
- **Compass Doll**: Tiny carved wooden doll that points toward a specific person (or location). Used to track Charlaine.
- **Summoning Circles**: Etched inscriptions that glow with azure light. Act as gateways. Found in the storage unit where the victim was discovered.
- **Glitter Bomb Ward**: A protective ward disguised as a harmless prank — triggered by opening sealed letters, it releases a cloud of multicolored glitter.
- **Rosettes**: Leather tokens with a stylized claw motif. Fangs and Fur's 50th-anniversary keepsakes. One was found at the storage unit crime scene, linking the bar to the case.
- **Different schools**: European continental magic (Krabat's tradition) and Voodoo are distinct systems. They don't mix easily.
