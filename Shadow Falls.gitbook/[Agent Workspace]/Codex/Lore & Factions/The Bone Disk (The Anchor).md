# Lore: The Bone Disk (The Anchor)

## Description
- A disk of carved bone, approximately six inches across.
- The surface is covered in the same geometric inscription that was cut into the concrete floor of unit eleven at Main Street Mini Storage — curves and angles, a pattern that repeats and folds back on itself.
- Unlike the floor inscription, this one is three-dimensional — carved in relief, the lines sharp and deep.
- Mounted on a wooden stand so it faces the room.
- Cold to the touch. "The specific cold of a thing that had been used as a doorway too many times."

## Function
- The anchor/instrument Legba described. Used to tear the membrane between the physical world and the spirit world.
- Every use leaves a scar on the membrane. Enough scars and the membrane fails entirely.
- The inscription in the storage unit was the receiving end of a working — the body (Frankie) was the marker, the blood was the seal.
- The bone disk is the transmitting end — the instrument that actually tears.

## Current Status
- Found in the underground chamber beneath the cabin in the woods.
- Was on a workbench, arranged with other ritual objects (iron bowl with residue, burned-down candles, binding cord).
- Currently in Jam's jacket pocket, wrapped in cloth.
- Krabat set up the cabin and left the disk there. It's unclear whether he wanted Jam to find it, or if he was compelled to leave it.

## Narrative Significance
- Returning this disk to Legba is the price for the location of the woman in the sepia photograph — the woman Jam has been searching for for 40 years.
- It is also evidence of whoever is behind the killings using Legba's old architecture.
- Possession of the disk likely makes Jam a target.
