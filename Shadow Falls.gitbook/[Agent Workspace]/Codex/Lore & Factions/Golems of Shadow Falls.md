# Lore: Golems of Shadow Falls

## Description & History
- **Summary**: Golems are humanoid constructs animated by magic and elemental forces. Jam Slate is the only known active golem in Shadow Falls. He's made of clay and has served as the town protector for over a century.
- **History & Origin**: Created by an unknown mage to protect Shadow Falls over 100 years ago. The circumstances of his creation are not fully explored. He has since assimilated into human society as the town Sheriff.

## Rules & Mechanics
- **Clay body**: Vulnerable to heat and dehydration. Hot sun causes his clay to crack, stiffen, and potentially crumble.
- **Rehydration**: Absorbs moisture through his clay skin. Coffee is the most effective. Lemonade and other liquids work. Water is essential for survival.
- **Regeneration**: Missing body parts (e.g., fingers) regenerate when properly rehydrated.
- **Strength**: Superhuman — can crack bones, withstand bullets (though they sting), and absorb physical damage.
- **Senses**: Smell, sight, hearing — all functional. Can detect magical residues like ozone and clove.
- **Clay manipulation**: Can seal bags with a thin layer of clay. Other clay manipulation abilities are implied but not fully explored.
- **Limitations**: Does not need sleep. Vulnerable to extreme drying. Magic (especially voodoo) affects him — he's superstitious about it.
