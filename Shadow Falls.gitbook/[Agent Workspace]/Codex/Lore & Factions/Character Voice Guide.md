# Character Voice Guide

## Jam Slate
- **Register**: Cynical, wisecracking, tough. Film noir detective filtered through a 100-year-old golem who's seen it all.
- **Vocabulary**: Uses "ain't," contractions freely. Colloquial. Calls people by name. Terse. Sarcastic. Understated humor.
- **Cadence**: Short sentences. Punches. Sometimes a longer sentence for atmosphere, then snaps back. Internal monologue is cynical observation, not emotional wallowing.
- **Tells**: Calls Anna "kiddo" (affectionate, protective). Refers to Legba as "Daddy Longlegs" (deliberately disrespectful). Uses dry metaphors sparingly ("like a sack of potatoes," "like greased piglets"). Avoids flowery language instinctually.
- **What he sounds like**: Raymond Chandler's Philip Marlowe, if Marlowe was made of clay and had been a small-town sheriff for a hundred years. Weary competence. Jokes to cope.
- **Do NOT**: Make him whiny, overly introspective, poetic, or sentimental. He's seen too much for that.

## Charlaine Meyers
- **Register**: Professional respect mixed with Creole warmth. More careful and diplomatic than Jam. Sharp. Protective of her heritage.
- **Tells**: French/Creole phrases slip out ("mon cher...iff," "la douleur exquise," "messieurs"). Chews gum when thinking. Quietly observant. Uses humor to needle Jam.
- **Dynamic with Jam**: Playful teasing (calls him out on his bullshit), deep loyalty. She's the only one who can get away with mocking him. Their banter is a sign of trust.
- **Do NOT**: Make her a damsel. She's resourceful, carries her grandmother's tools, has latent magical power in her bloodline.

## Anna Arborio
- **Register**: Motherly authority layered over vampire weariness. Formal when being mayor, warmer with Jam. Pragmatic to a fault.
- **Tells**: Vampire metaphors ("one day at a time," pig's blood flask, AA chip). Calls Jam by name. Exhaustion shows in her voice — hasn't slept since the killings started. Organizes when stressed.
- **Dynamic with Jam**: Sponsor/sponsee, mayor/sheriff, old friends. She's the closest thing to family Jam has. She worries. He dismisses her worry. They both know he's wrong.
- **Do NOT**: Make her frail or helpless. She's a vampire who's been sober 50 years. That takes more willpower than any magical strength.

## Krabat (Šajatović)
- **Register**: Formal, evasive, condescending. European mage-scholar. Chooses words carefully. Always sounds like he knows more than he's saying.
- **Tells**: Calls Charlaine "Charly." Hates being called "Šajatović." Silky, predatory speech patterns. Black eyes, unsettling calm. White hair, graceful movements contrast with the rough setting of Fangs and Fur.
- **Dynamic with Others**: Pure disdain for Jam. Respectful distance with Charlaine (she gets a nod). Professional relationship with Anna. He's useful but never trustworthy.
- **Do NOT**: Make him obviously villainous. He's a manipulator and a survivor, not a mustache-twirling evil mage.

## Papa Legba / Eshu
- **Register**: Ancient, amused, patient. Speaks in riddles and deals. Cryptic but not opaque — he's clear about the terms, just not the implications.
- **Tells**: "Names have power for me and my kin." Objects to "Daddy Longlegs." Accompanied by a giant red-eyed hound (the spirit-world guardian). His laugh is "all smoke and shadows."
- **Voice quality**: Rasps, dry-leaves-on-pavement. Borrowed voices when possessing someone. His laugh is unsettling.
- **Do NOT**: Make him a standard villain. He's a trickster. He keeps his word — exactly. The trick is in the wording, not the breaking of the deal.
