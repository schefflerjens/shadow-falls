# Worldbuilding & Magic Rules

## The Veil
- Shadow Falls is a small town where supernaturals live openly alongside humans — but no one talks about it. It's an unspoken agreement. Shifters run the docks. Trolls drink at Fangs and Fur. The mayor is a vampire. Locals know, tourists don't ask, outsiders are politely steered away.
- The wider human world is unaware of the supernatural. Government agencies like Herb Weatherby's exist to monitor and suppress evidence.
- Jam is publicly known as "the Sheriff." His clay skin draws stares from newcomers but locals wave it off. He's been around a century. Nobody questions it.

## Known Species
- **Golems**: Clay constructs animated by elemental magic. Only one active golem known (Jam). Vulnerable to heat/dehydration. Regenerate via moisture (coffee works fastest). Superhuman strength. Don't need sleep. Bullets sting but don't stop them.
- **Vampires**: Blood-dependent. Can survive on animal blood (pig's blood common). AA-like sobriety culture for reformed vampires. Sunlight weakens but doesn't kill. Can be possessed by Loa due to being "dead yet alive." Fangs, heightened senses, longevity.
- **Shifters**: Subtle physical tells (mentioned in passing at Fangs and Fur). Integrated into town life.
- **Trolls/Ogres**: Hulking, strong, durable. Grog is a regular at Fangs and Fur. Blue-collar, hard-drinking.
- **Mages (European tradition)**: Rare. Krabat is centuries old. His magic is formal, ritualistic, relies on symbols and relics. Different from Voodoo — the schools do not mix.
- **Flesh Golems**: The Murder Twins are mass-produced constructs — identical, forgettable faces, created via magic. They vanish like soap bubbles when their purpose ends (ozone + clove residue).

## Magic Rules
1. **Magic leaves traces**: Ozone + clove = teleportation signature. Lavender-tracking dust can follow residue up to 2-3 hours.
2. **Different schools don't mix**: European continental magic and Voodoo operate on different principles. A practitioner of one cannot easily use the other.
3. **Voodoo & Loa**: Operates through deals, intermediaries, and offerings. Loa (like Papa Legba) exist in the spirit world and cannot act directly in the physical realm without a vessel or a crossroads (place where life and death intersect). Deals are binding. Always a price.
4. **The Spirit World**: A parallel dimension. Vast empty void with shimmering walls. Guarded by giant red-eyed hounds. Accessible via summoning circles that glow azure. Time and space are distorted there.
5. **Wards & Talismans**: Wards can be disguised as mundane objects (e.g., glitter bomb in a letter). Compass-dolls track specific people. Tracking dust reveals magical residue. Items from a mambo carry power through connection to a Loa.
6. **Crossroads**: Places where life and death intersect — murder scenes, vampire bodies, graveyards. These thin the barrier between worlds and allow Loa to manifest.
7. **Flesh Golems**: Created in batches. Identical. Expendable. When deactivated, they dissolve or vanish rather than leaving bodies. Their creation requires a mage with knowledge of both European construct magic and some form of soul-animation.
