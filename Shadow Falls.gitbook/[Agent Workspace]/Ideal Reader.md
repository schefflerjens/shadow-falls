# Ideal Reader Persona

**Name:** Jens (the author, but reading as himself)
**Age:** 45+
**Reading Level:** Adult, experienced genre fiction reader

**Taste:**
- Loves noir detectives (Chandler, Hammett)
- Urban fantasy (Jim Butcher's *Dresden Files*, Richard Kadrey's *Sandman Slim*)
- Small-town supernatural settings
- Tight plotting and atmospheric prose
- A cynical voice that earns its emotional beats
- Protagonists who are genuinely vulnerable
- Stories where every choice has a cost

**Sensitivity:**
- Notices when pacing drags
- Spots weak or unclear character motivation
- Catches inconsistent voice or tone shifts
- Flags when supernatural rules aren't clearly established
- Aware of continuity errors (timeline, character knowledge between scenes)
- Cares about emotional authenticity — doesn't want shortcuts

**Expectations:**
- A noir detective voice that feels lived-in, not pasted on
- Supernatural elements with internal consistency
- Emotional stakes that land because they're earned, not announced
- A satisfying arc even in a serialized story
- Atmosphere that pulls you in and doesn't let go
