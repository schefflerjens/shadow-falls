Jam chases the killer twins across Shadow Falls rooftops in blistering heat, his clay body cracking. He corners one in an abandoned basement, subdues him, then a second twin appears and empties a magazine into him. Jam knocks him out too, but loses a finger to dehydration. He buys lemonade from a roadside stand to rehydrate — and the twins vanish via teleportation magic (ozone + clove residue). Jam realizes a mage is involved and the case just got nuclear.
