The tea ritual opened a line of communication with Legba (Ch7), but he can only speak through steam — not act. To complete the transaction and deliver the anchor to his domain, Mama Marie needs a crossroads for him to fully manifest. She calls Anna — a vampire, dead yet alive, qualifies as a living crossroads. Anna arrives (she volunteered for this). Mama Marie performs a possession ritual. Legba possesses Anna's body. He tries to declare the deal complete, arguing Charlaine's rescue satisfies their bargain. Jam counters: "someone" ≠ "something." Legba concedes, amused. Jam asks for a way to stop the source of the killings without more citizens harmed. Legba tells him to go to City Hall before dawn. Then Legba turns to Mama Marie — she owes him a price for meddling through the doll. He drinks her blood, turning her into a vampire. Charlaine insists on coming with Jam. They step outside. Jam's car is destroyed by the earlier lightning strike. Twenty-five minutes to sunrise. They run.
