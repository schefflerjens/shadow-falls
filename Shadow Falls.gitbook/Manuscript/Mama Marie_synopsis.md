Mama Marie's ritual succeeds — Papa Legba possesses Anna's body (she volunteered as a crossroads — vampire, dead yet alive). Legba tries to declare the deal complete but Jam outsmarts him on a technicality: "someone" ≠ "something." Legba concedes and Jam asks for a way to stop the source of killings without more citizens harmed. Legba tells him to go to City Hall before dawn. Then Legba turns on Mama Marie, drinking her blood as payment — turning her into a vampire. Marie collapses. Charlaine insists on coming with Jam. They step outside to find Jam's car destroyed by the earlier lightning strike. Twenty-five minutes to sunrise. They run.
