### Story Beats
- Jam walks into City Hall with the compass-doll in his pocket. Anna is in her office, the mess still everywhere. Jam sets the doll on her desk and explains everything that happened in the storage unit and the spirit world — the inscription, the void, the red-eyed hound, Legba's deal, the photograph of the woman he's been looking for for 40 years, the Jack of Crossroads card, and the revelation that Krabat has Charlaine.
- Anna listens without interrupting. When Jam finishes, she picks up the doll and examines it. She asks if Jam trusts Legba. Jam says no, but the deal is the only lead he has. The photograph is leverage Legba knows he can't ignore.
- Anna sets the doll on the desk. It rotates about forty-five degrees, pointing steadily toward the wall that faces the woods beyond the western edge of town. Anna picks up a petri dish and lays the doll inside it. She walks across the room. The doll keeps pointing in the same direction, like a homemade water compass.
- "Go bring them back," Anna says.
- Jam takes the petri dish and heads out. The doll points toward the woods beyond Shadow Falls.
