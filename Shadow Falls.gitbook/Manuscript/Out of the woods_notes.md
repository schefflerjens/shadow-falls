### Story Beats
- Charlaine won't wait. She's off the bench and pacing before Jam finishes his first mug of burnt coffee. The doll was Grand-Mère's work — the transformation, the diversion, the way it grew and walked and drew the constructs away. That wasn't Legba. That was her grandmother. Charlaine needs to know why, and she needs to know now.
- Jam puts the mug down. He has the bone disk in his pocket, cold against his ribs, and no idea what to do with it. Mama Marie is the only person in town who can read Legba's architecture. He needs answers if he's going to figure out what to do before the Bureau arrives.
- They take the car. The pharmacy is on the eastern edge of town, a narrow two-story wedged between a laundromat and a boarded-up barbershop. A light is on upstairs. Mama Marie is awake.
- She opens the door before Jam knocks. She's already dressed — not the robe of someone who was sleeping. She looks at Charlaine first, then at Jam, then at the shape of the disk in his jacket pocket. She knows why they're there.
- Inside: the pharmacy counter in front, the living quarters in back. Mama Marie leads them through to the kitchen. She sets water to boil without asking. The room smells of dried herbs and candle wax.
- Jam sets the bone disk on the table. Mama Marie looks at it for a long moment before touching it. She confirms it's Legba's architecture — old crossroads workings, repurposed by someone who knows what they're doing but doesn't care what they're tearing. The disk is the instrument. The inscriptions in town are the doors. Each murder was a key turning in a lock.
- Charlaine asks about the doll. Mama Marie confirms she put herself into it — a watching charm, keyed to Charlaine's blood. She's been aware of the working since the moment Charlaine was taken. She couldn't act directly (that's not her domain), but she could watch. When the moment came, she pushed the doll to transform.
- This reveals that Charlaine's bloodline has dormant power. The doll responded to her, not to Jam. Whatever the killer is doing, it's connected to her — she was never a random target.
- Charlaine absorbs this. She asks what they do next. Mama Marie says the disk is the key — as long as Jam has it, whoever's behind the workings will come for him. They need to be ready.
- Mama Marie offers to try reaching Legba through a tea ritual. It's not the same as the void — it's a line of communication, not a door. But if Legba wants to speak, he'll hear the call.
- Jam agrees. Mama Marie prepares the leaves.
