With Charlaine missing, Jam returns to the storage unit where the latest victim was found. He discovers a magical inscription etched into the concrete floor beneath dried blood. Tracing it triggers an azure summoning circle that transports him to the spirit world. There, a giant red-eyed hound guards the void, and a hooded figure calling himself Eshu (Papa Legba) offers Jam a deal: find someone for him, and Legba will give Jam something he's been searching for.
