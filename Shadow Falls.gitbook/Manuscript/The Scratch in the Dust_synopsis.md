With Charlaine missing and no other leads, Jam returns to the Main Street Mini Storage — the scene of the latest murder. Under dried blood, he finds a magical inscription in the concrete floor. Tracing it triggers an azure summoning circle that pulls him into the spirit world. There, a giant red-eyed hound guards the void, and Eshu (Papa Legba) offers a deal: find who is using his old workings to tear the membrane between worlds and bring him the anchor they're using. In return, Legba will reveal the location of a woman in a sepia photograph — someone Jam has been searching for for 40 years. Legba gives Jam the Jack of Crossroads to cross between worlds, and reveals that Krabat has Charlaine. When Jam exits the void back into the storage unit, he finds a small wooden compass-doll on the floor, etched with Charlaine's name — pointing the way.
