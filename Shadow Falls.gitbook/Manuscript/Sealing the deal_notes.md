### Story Beats
- Twenty-five minutes to sunrise. Jam and Charlaine are on foot — Jam's car is a smoking wreck from the lightning strike. They sprint toward the silhouette of City Hall against the rising sun.
- The air smells of rain and burning rubber. The first rays of dawn paint the sky in orange and pink. Panting and exhausted, they reach the steps just as the clock tower chimes.
- The heavy oak doors are locked. Jam rattles them — nothing.
- A commotion distracts them. A short, middle-aged man in a rumpled suit stumbles out of an alleyway, pursued by three snarling stray dogs. Charlaine grabs a loose brick from a planter and throws it — scatters the dogs. The man scrambles to his feet, clutching a battered briefcase.
- He introduces himself as Herbert "Herb" Weatherby. He's here on behalf of the Mayor — or rather, on behalf of the agency Anna contacted. He's flustered, grateful for the rescue.
- Herb works for a clandestine government agency that monitors supernatural activity. His superiors dismissed Shadow Falls as a "small-town spook story." He went through classified files on his own time and found something he thinks is relevant.
- Jam is skeptical. "You're a paper pusher. What can you do?"
- Charlaine reminds him about the map. The map Legba told them to bring to City Hall. Jam pulls it from his jacket — the crude sketch he found in the cabin, with the address written in a language he couldn't read.
- Herb recognizes it. It's an old city survey with cryptic markings — arcane symbols and hidden lore. He deciphers a section, pointing them toward a specific location within the city.
- A tentative alliance forms. Jam doesn't trust Herb, but the decoded map is the first solid lead they've had since the anchor. Charlaine is practical — they have no time to waste.
