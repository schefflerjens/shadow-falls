### Story Beats
- Twenty-five minutes to sunrise. Jam and Charlaine are on foot — Jam's car is a smoking wreck from the lightning strike. They sprint toward the silhouette of City Hall against the rising sun.
- The air smells of rain and burning rubber. Panting and exhausted, they reach the steps just as the clock tower chimes.
- The heavy oak doors are locked. Jam rattles them — nothing.
- A commotion in the alley beside the building. A short, middle-aged man in a rumpled suit stumbles out, pursued by three snarling stray dogs. Charlaine grabs a loose brick from a planter and throws it — scatters the dogs. The man scrambles to his feet, clutching a battered briefcase.
- He introduces himself as Herbert "Herb" Weatherby. He works for the clandestine supernatural monitoring agency that Anna contacted. His superiors dismissed Shadow Falls as a "small-town spook story." He came anyway, on his own time.
- Herb went through classified files and mapped the murder locations against an old city survey. The crime scenes trace a precise geometric pattern — the same geometry Jam saw in the storage unit inscription. One point on the map hasn't been activated yet: the site of the final working.
- Jam is skeptical. "You drove here alone with a theory?"
- Herb admits he's not authorized. He tried to tell his superiors. They told him to stay at his desk. He drove anyway.
- Charlaine looks at the map. She recognizes the location — it's a place in Shadow Falls she knows.
- A tentative alliance forms. Jam doesn't trust Herb, but the decoded map is the first solid lead since the anchor. The geometry matches what Legba described. If they're right, stopping the final working means stopping the membrane from failing completely.
- No time to waste. The Bureau arrives today. Krabat is moving before dawn.

### 🔄 Continuity Note
The map is not from the cabin (that was never written). It's from Herb's agency files — he cross-referenced the crime scene locations against the original city survey and found the unactivated point. This matches Legba's clue that "what you need" would be at City Hall.
