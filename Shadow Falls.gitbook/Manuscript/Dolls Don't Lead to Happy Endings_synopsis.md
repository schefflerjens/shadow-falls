Jam follows the compass-doll deep into the woods, finding a dilapidated cabin guarded by half a dozen identical murder twins (flesh golems). He fights through them, discovers a secret passage beneath a loose floorboard, and finds Charlaine bound in an underground chamber. He frees her and they escape the cabin, but the twins pursue. The doll transforms into a life-sized copy of Charlaine that creates a diversion, allowing Jam and Charlaine to reach his car and flee back to Shadow Falls.
