Jam and Charlaine return to the basement crime scene. Charlaine uses her grandmother's tracking dust to find magical residue, uncovering a worn Fangs and Fur 50th-anniversary rosette. They take the lead to the bar. Inside, Jam questions Millie, Charlaine shows the rosette around, and Krabat appears — evasive and cryptic. Jam defuses a bar fight with free drinks. When he turns back, Charlaine and Krabat have vanished. Ozone and clove linger.
