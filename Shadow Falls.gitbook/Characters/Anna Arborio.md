# Character Profile: Anna Arborio

## Role & Narrative Purpose
- **Narrative Function**: Key character in Shadow Falls.
- **Core Narrative Conflict**: Supernatural mysteries and investigation.

## Description & Background
Mayor and town doctor of Shadow Falls, Anna has a complicated relationship with Jam. On the one hand, Jam is an old friend and her AA sponsor. On the other hand, Jam's need to protect the town and Anna's ambitions for the community are sometimes at odds. Anna has pitch black hair, very fair skin, and bloodshot eyes that she usually hides behind sunglasses.
